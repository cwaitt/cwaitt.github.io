Projects and Resources

This page collects research projects, teaching materials, workshops, coding resources, and student-facing project archives.

Some items are polished resources, while others are active or developing projects. The goal is to make useful materials easier to find for students, collaborators, and anyone interested in chemistry, computation, data science, and undergraduate research.

Featured Projects

Cheminformatics Active

Molecular Structure to Flavor

A cheminformatics project focused on connecting molecular structure to reported flavor or taste labels. This project uses molecular datasets, SMILES strings, RDKit descriptors, functional group analysis, and interpretable machine learning workflows.

The long-term goal is to help students explore how chemical structure relates to sensory properties such as sweet, bitter, sour, salty, umami, and other flavor descriptors.

Bioinformatics In Development

Molecular Structure to Flavor Receptor Triggering

A developing bioinformatics direction focused on how molecular structure may relate to the activation of taste or flavor-related receptors.

This project is intended to create a biology-friendly entry point into coding, molecular datasets, receptor information, sequence or structure data, and structure-function relationships.

LaTeX and Overleaf Workshop

Introductory workshop materials for students learning LaTeX, Overleaf, document structure, mathematical equations, figures, tables, citations, and scientific writing.

This workshop grew out of my work as a Navari Family Center for Digital Scholarship Fellow at the University of Notre Dame.
